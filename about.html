About Sunrise Community Clinic

Founded to serve the growing Mukono community, Sunrise Community Clinic has provided outpatient and preventive health services for families of all backgrounds.

Our Mission

Accessible Healthcare

We believe quality healthcare should be within reach for every family, regardless of income.

Compassionate Staff

Every member of our team is trained to treat patients with dignity, patience, and respect.
