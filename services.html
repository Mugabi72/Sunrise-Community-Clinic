Our Services

We offer a range of outpatient and preventive services for individuals and families.

General Consultations

Routine checkups and diagnosis for common illnesses, led by our clinical staff.

Maternal & Child Health

Antenatal checkups, immunizations, and growth monitoring for infants and young children.

Nursing Care

Wound dressing, injections, and basic in-clinic nursing support administered by qualified nurses.

Health Screening

Blood pressure, blood sugar, and general wellness screening for early detection of health issues.

Ready to book a visit? Contact us to schedule an appointment.

Please Note

For medical emergencies, please visit the nearest hospital emergency department directly.
